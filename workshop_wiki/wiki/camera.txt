# The Wiki's pages: the view, pictures, time (workshop_wiki/src/written.rs).

== frame
> Frames those named (or a place, or both: frame pikachu piplup left) once, whole, above the dialogue box, the view gliding there; snap: at once, a cut; follows: keeps them framed as they move. One framing per moment, as in the game's scenes.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
frame pikachu piplup
pikachu: Smile!

== glide
> A light move of the view, slow at its ends, to someone or a place: glide to piplup, glide to right in 2s.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup put right
pikachu: Is someone over there?
glide to piplup in 2s
piplup: Hi!

== moment
> Opens a moment of the scene, with its title, until the next one: one framing per moment, as in the game's scenes.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
moment: the arrival
frame pikachu piplup
pikachu: Hello!

== memory
> A memory, up to its end: in sepia between two white fades, as an old picture, then back to the present.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: I remember that day...
memory
  piplup (happy): Let's be friends!
end
pikachu: Good times.

== together
> Starts the lines up to end at the same time; the film goes on once the longest is over.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
together
  pikachu jump
  piplup jump
end
pikachu: Again!

== beside
> Plays its lines one after the other alongside the film, which goes on; named, wait name waits for them.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
beside hops
  pikachu jump
  pikachu jump
end
piplup: Look at him go.
wait hops

== at
> Plays a line at a moment: of the scene (at 2s), of a mark (at hello + 1s), or from here (at +2s). The film does not wait for it.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
at 2s: piplup jump
pikachu: In two seconds, he jumps.
wait 2s

== mark
> Marks this instant of the scene by a name, for at name + 2s.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
mark hello
at hello + 1s: piplup jump
pikachu: One, two...
wait 2s

== pace
> The rhythm of what follows: pace slow or pace brisk makes the waits said after it (wait 2s) longer or shorter, pace 1.5 by so much, pace normal as written; a word's own time stays its own. pace lines 2s lets each line said go on by itself after two seconds; pace lines off waits for the reader again.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pace slow
pikachu: Shh... listen.
wait 1s
pace lines 2s
piplup: The waves...
pikachu: ...are singing.
pace lines off
pace normal
piplup jump

== when
> Plays a line (or a block up to end) as soon as something happens: when pikachu arrives, when pikachu leaves… The film does not wait for it. Before a fight, when health(a) <= 50 plays lines once that much health is left.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu walk to right &
when pikachu arrives: pikachu jump
piplup: Go, go!
wait pikachu
