== status
> The game's status icon over someone, as in a dungeon: sleep, burn, poison, frozen, confused, cringe…; status off takes it away. A stat up or down: the game's own arrows over them.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup status sleep
pikachu: He fell asleep...
pikachu status attack up


== ghost
> A ghost: half visible, he floats.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu ghost
piplup (worried): Pikachu...?
