# The Wiki's pages: effects, light, weather (workshop_wiki/src/written.rs).

== effect
> A move's own effect of the game, by the move's name: on someone, at a place, or from one to another, flying.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
effect Thunderbolt from pikachu to piplup
piplup (pain): Ouch!

== flash
> The screen flashes white (or in a colour said), with the game's own sound: a shock, a revelation.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: I've got it!
flash
piplup (surprised): What was that?

== daytime
> The light of a time of day over the scene: morning, day, evening or night, changing in 2s (or as long as said); stars: the stars twinkle over it.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
daytime night stars
pikachu: Look at the stars.

== weather
> The game's weather over the scene: rain, snow, sand, fog, hail or sun; heavy, under a storm's light. weather off: fair again.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
weather rain heavy
pikachu (worried): A storm is coming.
weather off

== aura
> An ambience of someone's type, only when asked, in sharp pixels: a few cinders about a fire kind, a spark off an electric kind when angry, a drop off a water kind when scared, leaves off a grass kind when it dances. aura off: none.
scene: beach charmander pikachu
aura on charmander
aura on pikachu
pikachu (angry): Who took my berry?
charmander (worried): Not me!
aura off charmander
