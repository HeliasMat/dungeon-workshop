# The Wiki's pages: the Body family, what the body does where it stands (workshop_wiki/src/written.rs).

== pose
> Someone plays one of their sprite's animations, by a clear name: sit, lie, cringe, stagger, look_up, proud, breathe, attack, charge, doze, and the community's own (dance, twirl, show_off, wave, withdraw, hover, punch, kick, bite, tiptoe, knocked_out…): once (then standing again), loop (again and again) or hold (staying on its last picture); each has its own default. pose off: standing again.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu pose sit
piplup: Tired?
pikachu (happy): A little.
pikachu pose off

== sit
> Someone sits down and stays sitting, with their sprite's Sit animation (lying down if their sprite has none, else asleep). Like every gesture word: once, loop or hold to play it otherwise; pose off, or any move, stands them again.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu sit
piplup: Tired already?
pikachu (happy): Just resting.
pikachu pose off

== lie_down

== push
> Two shove each other, as in sumo: they lock, strain and tremble, then one throws the other back and he falls. The heavier wins, by the game's own weights (the one who pushes if they are not known); wins says who.

pikachu put left
munchlax put right
pikachu (determined): Out of the ring!
pikachu push munchlax wins pikachu
munchlax (pain): Oof...
