# The Wiki's pages: fights (workshop_wiki/src/written.rs).

== fight
> A fight, played by the fights' own rules, with the game's moves, bars and log: who... vs who...; a duel by default, double for two against two; boss, the foe looms first; level n. fight directed … end tells one by one, turn by turn.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
fight pikachu vs piplup level 10
pikachu: Good fight!

== join
> Someone joins the team: the game's sentence, a hop.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup join team
pikachu: Welcome aboard!

== evolve
> Someone evolves into another species: the white light, the pulsing silhouette, the new form and its sentence. Their lines then show the new name and portrait. With no species, the next of their family, as the game's tables say; one not of their family is remarked.
scene: beach eevee pichu
eevee evolve umbreon
pichu: Wow!
pichu evolve
pikachu: Me too!

== transform
> Someone transforms into another, of the scene or a species not there: the evolution's light, without its sentence, and they look like the other while staying themselves: their name, portrait and lines are their own. transform into its next form: the next of their family. transform back: themselves again.
scene: beach ditto pikachu
ditto put center
pikachu put left
ditto transform into pikachu
ditto: Two of us!
ditto transform back
pikachu (surprised): Wow.

== attack
> Someone uses a move of the game, by its name, as the game shows it: their own animation, the move's effects and sound, the target knocked back and hurt. No bars, no numbers: fight does those.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu attack Thunderbolt at piplup
piplup (pain): Ouch!

== roar
> A boss's cry: the game's roar (it has no cry of each species), the view shaking, harder with hard; the sprite's own Roar animation where it has one (Dialga, Palkia, Entei...), else its Charge and a rise.
scene: beach pikachu groudon
pikachu put left
groudon put right
groudon roar hard
pikachu (surprised): Whoa!

== charge
> Gathering power: their Charge animation, the game's shine of power rising (its stats-up effect) and its sound, held a beat. With a move told, their next attack with that move lands with a flash and a hard shake, the target knocked further.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu charge Thunderbolt
pikachu attack Thunderbolt at piplup

== dodge
> A quick hop aside, left, right or back (back by default), with their Hop animation. Right after an attack at them, or right before one, the blow misses: they hop aside just as it lands, no knock back, "Missed!" over them.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu attack Tackle at piplup
piplup dodge left
piplup: Too slow!

== finish
> The finishing blow: time slowed, the screen white, the move; the one struck faints, and the music falls silent a moment over them.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu finish piplup Thunderbolt

== use
> A move out of a fight, by its name, as the game shows it: they turn to the one it is used on (or to the point), their own animation, the move's effects and sound. No one is knocked back nor hurt, nothing missed: Protect, Hypnosis, Sunny Day, a move shown off. No move, or its move: one their kind learns by their level, of their own type if it can. A move their kind never learns is remarked, and plays all the same.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
piplup use Bubble on pikachu
pikachu (dizzy): Bubbles... everywhere...
pikachu use its move
