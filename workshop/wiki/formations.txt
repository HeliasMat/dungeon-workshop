# The Wiki's pages: the Formations family, several standing in a shape (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== form
> Several stand in a shape round a place (the middle without one): side by side (row), one behind the other (column), in a ring facing its middle (ring), or side by side facing us (lineup). At the head of a scene they stand there; later on they walk there, or run with run (walk walks them there even at the head). look left, look piplup turn them all one way or to someone.
scene: beach pikachu piplup eevee
form ring pikachu piplup eevee center
pikachu: Is everyone here?
form lineup pikachu piplup eevee to center run
eevee (happy): Ready!
form row pikachu piplup eevee to left look right
