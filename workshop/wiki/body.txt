# The Wiki's pages: the Body family, what the body does where it stands (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== pose
> Someone plays one of their sprite's animations, by a clear name: sit, lie, cringe, stagger, look_up, proud, breathe, attack, charge, doze… once (then standing again), loop (again and again) or hold (staying on its last picture); each has its own default. pose off: standing again.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu pose sit
piplup: Tired?
pikachu (happy): A little.
pikachu pose off

== sleep
> Someone falls asleep, lying down with the game's Z's, until sleep off (their Wake animation).
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu sleep
piplup: Good night.
wait 1s
pikachu sleep off
pikachu (happy): Morning!

== eat
> Someone eats what they hold, with their Eat animation: bites, and the thing shrinks away.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu hold Apple
pikachu eat Apple
pikachu (joyous): Yum!

== hurt
> Someone is hurt: their Pain animation, knocked back the way they look; back sets how far, hard knocks them further and shakes the view.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup (angry): Take that!
pikachu hurt hard
pikachu (pain): Ouch!

== tremble
> Someone trembles where they stand, until tremble off.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu tremble
pikachu (worried): It's so cold...
pikachu tremble off
piplup: Here, take my scarf.

== fall
> Someone falls and stays down: over (their Tumble animation, the default), back (on their back) or trip (tripping forward).
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu walk to center
pikachu fall trip
piplup (surprised): Are you all right?

== faint
> Someone faints, with their Faint animation, and stays down until faint off, when they get up again.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu faint
piplup (worried): Pikachu!
pikachu faint off
pikachu: I'm fine...

== cower
> Someone cowers: they cringe, sweat and tremble, facing what they fear if from says it.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup (angry): Grrr!
pikachu cower from piplup
pikachu (worried): Please don't...

== hug
> Someone runs up to the other: they face each other and hop for joy together.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu (joyous): You're back!
pikachu hug piplup
piplup (happy): I missed you!

== comfort
> Someone comes close to another who is down, nods to them, and the other takes heart.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup (sad): I lost it...
pikachu comfort piplup
pikachu: We'll find it together.

== cheer
> Someone cheers: two hops for joy, and their proud pose.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: We won!
pikachu cheer
pikachu (joyous): Hooray!

== carry
> Someone lifts another over their head: the other hops up and is carried there, wherever they go, turned with them.
scene: beach machamp pikachu
machamp put center
pikachu put left
machamp carry pikachu
machamp walk to right
pikachu (happy): I can see the sea!

== put_down
> Someone puts down the one they carry: a hop down, before them, or to the place told.
scene: beach machamp pikachu
machamp put center
pikachu put left
machamp carry pikachu
machamp put_down pikachu to left

== shiver
> Someone shivers, with cold or fear: they tremble, a drop of sweat.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu shiver
pikachu (worried): It's cold tonight...

== stretch
> Someone stretches: up to the sky, then a deep breath. Where their sprite has no such animation, the nearest it has plays.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu stretch
pikachu (happy): What a morning!

== celebrate
> Someone celebrates: a spin, happy hops, their proud pose.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: We won!
pikachu celebrate

== push
> Two shove each other, as in sumo: they lock, strain and tremble, then one throws the other back and he falls. The heavier wins, by the game's own weights (the one who pushes if they are not known); wins says who.
scene: beach pikachu munchlax
pikachu put left
munchlax put right
pikachu (determined): Out of the ring!
pikachu push munchlax wins pikachu
munchlax (pain): Oof...

== tug
> Someone takes hold of another and pulls him along, walking back; the other is dragged after him and staggers.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu: Come on, this way!
pikachu tug piplup
piplup (surprised): Hey!

== tickle
> Someone tickles another, who laughs and wriggles.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu tickle piplup
piplup (happy): Ha ha, stop it!

== chomp
> Someone bites another: a snap of the head, the other hurt and shocked.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup (angry): Give it back!
piplup chomp pikachu
pikachu (pain): Ow!

== high_five
> Two meet face to face and jump up together, glad: a high five.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu (happy): We did it!
pikachu high_five piplup

== boost
> Someone crouches and gives another a leg up: the other climbs on his head and springs off, there (to where) or before him.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: Up you go!
pikachu boost piplup to 400 150
piplup (happy): I can see the sea!

== lift
> Someone lifts another up over his head, with an effort, and holds him there; put_down sets him down.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: I can't see!
pikachu lift piplup
piplup (happy): Much better!
pikachu put_down piplup
