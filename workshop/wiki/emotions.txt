# The Wiki's pages: the Emotions family, the game's emote balloons (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== emote
> One of the game's emote balloons over someone, with its sound: exclaim, shock, sweat, joy, laugh, question, notice, anger, munch. silent leaves the sound out; emote off takes it away.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: Pikachu, behind you!
pikachu emote exclaim
pikachu: What?
piplup emote laugh silent
piplup (happy): Just kidding!

== react
> Someone reacts as the game's people do: the emote and its sound, then the hop. joy, surprise, anger, fear, doubt or relief.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: I found your scarf!
pikachu react joy
pikachu (joyous): Thank you!

== sigh
> Someone sighs: a deep breath, and a drop of sweat.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: The rain again...
pikachu sigh
pikachu (sad): We stay in today.
