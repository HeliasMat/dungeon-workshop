# The Wiki's pages: the Turning family, where someone looks (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== face
> Someone turns to a side (left, right, up, down…), to someone, or round (away, their back to where they looked); fast turns at once; slow goes through each way in between, a step at a time, as the game's slow turns.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: Pikachu?
pikachu face left
wait 1s
pikachu face piplup slow
pikachu (sad): Leave me alone.

== look
> Someone looks around, as the game's routine (a glance one way, a longer one the other, then back), or to a side (left, right, up, down).
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu (worried): Did you hear that?
pikachu look around
pikachu look up
piplup: Up there!

== nod
> Someone nods yes, with their sprite's Nod animation; times nods again.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: Shall we go?
pikachu nod times 2
pikachu (happy): Let's go!

== shake_head
> Someone shakes their head no, as the game does it: quick turns of the head one way, the other, and back.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: Are you scared?
pikachu shake_head
pikachu (determined): Not at all!
