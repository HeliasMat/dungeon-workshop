# The Wiki's pages: fishing, at sea (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).
# A fisher's rod is drawn on a variant (pikachu_pecheur, painted from your own copy
# of the game); with a plain Pokémon the line plays without the rod, and the
# Script says so: the examples use Pikachu, so they play everywhere.

== release
> He lets his catch go: it thanks him and jumps back into the water.
uses fishing
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu cast at 330 152
bite
pikachu reel catch magikarp
pikachu release

== bite
> A fish bites the line in the water (the last one cast): the bobber dives, a splash, the fisher starts. bite strong: it is yanked under, the rod pulls him forward; bite nibble: it only touches, the bobber quivers twice and he wonders.
uses fishing
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu cast at 330 152
bite nibble
pikachu: Hm?
bite strong
pikachu (surprised): A big one!
pikachu reel catch magikarp

== reel
> He winds the line in on the reel, one click per turn: the bobber comes back to his feet. reel catch and what bit: it flies out of the water and lands beside him; reel snap: the line snaps; reel lose: the fish gets away, the line comes back empty.
uses fishing
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu cast at 330 152
bite
pikachu reel lose
pikachu (sad): It got away...
pikachu cast at 330 152
bite
pikachu reel catch magikarp
pikachu (joyous): Got it!

== cast
> A fisher casts his line: his swing, the line flying out, the bobber landing on the water (there, or before him). The rod is drawn on a fisher's variant of his sprite.
uses fishing
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu cast at 330 140
pikachu: Come on, fishies...
