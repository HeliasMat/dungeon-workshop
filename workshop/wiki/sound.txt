# The Wiki's pages: sounds and music (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== sound
> Plays a sound of the game (or a move's, or one of ours): on someone, when they are hit; loop, over and over, until sound name off.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
sound chirp loop
pikachu: Listen... birds.
sound chirp off

== music
> Plays a song of the game: fade 2s brings it in softly, volume 0.5 plays it softer; music off stops it, music off fade 2s lets it die away.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
music peace fade 2s
pikachu: What a quiet evening.
music off fade 2s
wait 2s
