# The Wiki's pages: moving about, crowds (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== wait
> Waits: so long (1s, 30f), until someone has arrived (wait pikachu), until what plays beside under that name ends, or until everything has stopped (wait all).
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu walk to right &
wait 1s
piplup: Wait for me!
wait pikachu

== collisions
> collisions on: walls, the ground and bodies stop people until the end of the scene (walks go round them); off: everyone goes straight through, as written.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
collisions on
pikachu walk to right
collisions off
