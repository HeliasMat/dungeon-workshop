# The Wiki's pages: the Movement family, going about (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== walk
> Someone walks to a place or to someone, step by step: slow or brisk sets the pace, backward walks with their back to where they go, via a b goes by those places first. The film waits until they get there; end the line with & to go on at once. Alone (lapras walk), it keeps someone on their feet whatever their kind: no hovering, swimming nor going through walls.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu walk to center brisk
piplup walk to pikachu slow
pikachu walk to left backward
piplup: There you are!

== run
> Like walk, at a run: someone runs to a place (or to someone), and the film waits until they arrive.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu run to piplup
piplup (surprised): Whoa, slow down!

== shift
> Someone slides a little without walking, still looking the same way: to a place, or by so many pixels (by 16 0), to make room or be pushed aside.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: Move over a bit.
piplup shift by 24 0
piplup (worried): Sorry!

== jump
> Someone leaps up, higher than a hop, as the game moves a body up and down: on the spot, or to a place or someone (jump to right_of piplup); height sets how high.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu (joyous): Watch this!
pikachu jump to right_of piplup height 24
piplup (surprised): Wow!

== hop
> The game's little hop, its gesture number one: the sprite's Hop animation; happy, surprised or angry add the game's emote and its sound (angry: two quick hops); times repeats it.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu (happy): We did it!
pikachu hop happy times 2
piplup hop surprised
piplup: Really?

== follow
> Someone follows another wherever they go, a step behind, until follow off.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup follow pikachu
pikachu walk to right
pikachu: Still there?
piplup follow off
piplup: Yes!

== chase
> Someone runs after another, keeping on their heels, until chase off.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
piplup chase pikachu
pikachu run to right
piplup chase off
pikachu (worried): Stop it!

== flee
> Someone runs away from another: to a place, or a little way off.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup (angry): Come here, you!
pikachu flee from piplup to left
pikachu (worried): No way!

== wander
> Someone strolls about where they are, now and then, until wander off.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu wander
piplup: Nice day for a walk.
wait 2s
pikachu wander off
pikachu: It is!

== ride
> Someone hops onto another's back (or head, with head) and rides there: they go where it goes, turn with it as it walks, rise and sink with it. How high they sit is the mount's own, from the game's points of its body; high and behind set it by hand.
scene: beach pikachu lapras
pikachu put left
lapras put center
pikachu ride on lapras
lapras walk to right
pikachu (happy): Faster!

== get_off
> Someone gets off the back they ride: a hop down, beside the mount, or to the place told.
scene: beach pikachu lapras
pikachu put left
lapras put center
pikachu ride on lapras
lapras walk to right
pikachu get_off to center

== swim
> Someone is in the water and swims: a third of their body under it, the water's line waving on them, a little bob. Alone, where they are; to a place, straight across the water, rings in their wake; from x y, coming in from there.
scene: beach lapras pikachu
lapras put 330 95
pikachu put 300 175
lapras swim
lapras swim to 460 90
pikachu (happy): Show off!

== dive
> Someone in the water dives under it: a little jump, a splash, down through the water and gone, bubbles where they were; at a place, they swim there first.
scene: beach lapras pikachu
lapras put 330 95
pikachu put 300 175
lapras swim
lapras dive
pikachu (surprised): Where did it go?

== surface
> Someone under the water comes up again, where they dived or at the place told, with a splash.
scene: beach lapras pikachu
lapras put 330 95
pikachu put 300 175
lapras swim
lapras dive
wait 1s
lapras surface at 400 100
pikachu (happy): There you are!

== get_out
> Someone in the water hops out onto the shore, the nearest free ground to the place told (beside them, if none is), and walks again.
scene: beach lapras pikachu
lapras put 330 95
pikachu put 300 175
lapras swim
lapras get_out to 360 175
pikachu: Welcome back.

== fly
> Someone takes off and flies, bobbing in the air, their shadow on the ground under them: so high (24 pixels; height h), or to a place, straight over the walls. At the head of a scene, they are up there before it shows.
scene: lake pidgey pikachu
pidgey put 300 330
pikachu put 260 330
pidgey fly
pidgey fly to 90 330
pikachu (surprised): Over the cliff!

== land
> Someone flying comes down with their landing and walks again: where they are, or at the place told, flying there first.
scene: lake pidgey pikachu
pidgey put 300 330
pikachu put 260 330
pidgey fly
pidgey land at 300 400
pikachu: Nice landing.
