# The Wiki's pages written by hand: what a word does, said for an author,
# and a little film of our own as its example (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).
# A page: `== word`, its `> ` lines, then its example (a `scene:` in it).
# A word with no page here gets one made from its forms.
#
# This file: speaking, the film's own lines (scene, title, blocks).

== scene
> Opens a scene: a place, and who is there from the start. They are placed on their own, spread out and turned towards each other; everything until the next scene happens there.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu (happy): What a nice day!
piplup: Let's go for a swim.

== title
> The film's title, shown alone on the dark before its first scene.
title The Big Swim
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: Ready?

== transition
> How a scene goes to the next, written just before it: fade (to black, the default), white, boss (the white flashes before a boss), wipe, iris (the circle closes on the last one who spoke) or cut. With in or out it plays now, in the middle of a scene: transition fade out 1s, then transition fade in.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: Let's go home.
transition iris
scene: beach_evening pikachu piplup
piplup: Home at last.

== define
> Makes a recipe of your own: a name for a few lines, called later like any word. What follows its name are the words it is given.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
define greet who:pokemon
  jump who
  emote who joy
end
pikachu greet
piplup greet

== repeat
> Plays the lines up to its end so many times.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
repeat 3
  pikachu jump
end
piplup: Show-off!

== else
> In an if block, the lines played when the test is false.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
if chance(50)
  pikachu: Heads!
else
  pikachu: Tails!
end

== end
> Closes a block: a recipe, a repeat, together, beside, a choice, a memory…
scene: beach pikachu piplup
together
  pikachu jump
  piplup jump
end

== choice
> The player chooses an answer: one option line each, the lines under it played if it is chosen, then the film comes back together after end.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: Shall we swim?
choice
option Yes!
  piplup (joyous): Last one in is a Slowpoke!
option Not now
  piplup (sad): Oh, all right...
end

== popup
> Words that float up from someone and fade, like a number of damage or a little "+1".
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu jump
pikachu popup white +1

== bubble
> A speech bubble over someone, without the dialogue box: the film goes on at once.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu bubble Look!
piplup walk to center

== box
> Where the dialogue box stands. auto, as each scene starts: where it hides no one who speaks, as the game has it, at the foot of the screen and at the top for a line of someone standing low. top or bottom: kept there for the rest of the scene, whoever speaks.
scene: town pikachu piplup
piplup walk to 360 290
pikachu: Piplup? Where are you?
piplup: Down here!
box top
pikachu: The box stays up here now.
box auto
pikachu: And back where it hides no one.

== think
> Someone thinks a line: in the box, with their portrait and its face, the words in brackets as the game writes a thought. Nobody around hears it.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: Pikachu, look at the sea!
pikachu think (worried): It looks so cold...
pikachu (happy): It's lovely!

== narrate
> Words of no one's: in the box with no name nor portrait (narrate:, or narrate box:), centred on the screen over the scene (narrate screen:), or read on a sign, centred in the box (narrate sign:).
scene: beach pikachu piplup
narrate screen: The next morning...
narrate: The sea was calm.
narrate sign: Beware of the waves!
pikachu (worried): Waves?

== explain
> Someone takes the floor to explain, as the game's explaining routine: stepping on the spot a moment, then standing still. Their lines follow.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup (surprised): How does the tide work?
pikachu explain
pikachu (happy): Twice a day, the sea comes up the beach!

== whisper
> Someone tells the other a secret: they step up close, the other listens, notices something, and nods. The words follow, said low.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu whisper piplup
pikachu (worried): ...Don't tell anyone.
piplup: Promise.
