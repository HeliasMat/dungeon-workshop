# The Wiki's pages: the Presence family, who is on the stage and how they come and go (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== put
> Sets someone in a place, turned that way (facing a side, someone or a spot). At the head of a scene they are simply there; later on, they walk in from the nearest edge. Also puts things down: put object chest front_of pikachu.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left facing right
piplup put right facing pikachu
pikachu: You're so far away!

== spot
> Names a place of your own (x y), to use like any spot afterwards: put, walk to, frame.
spot rock 300 200
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu walk to rock
pikachu: My favourite rock.

== enter
> Someone comes in from outside the place, from a side or by an entrance, up to a spot (the middle without to). They are not there before this line; several come in one after the other.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: Where is he?
piplup enter from right to center
piplup: Sorry I'm late!

== exit
> Someone leaves by the nearest way on that side (or by that entrance) and is gone once out. Several leave in single file; the film waits for the last one.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: See you tomorrow!
piplup exit via right
pikachu (sad): Bye...

== appear
> Someone appears where they are told, as the game shows it: in its puff of smoke (smoke, the default), fading in (fade), in a white flash (flash) or in a vision (vision). Not someone walking in: enter does that.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu: Where did he go?
piplup appear left_of pikachu smoke
pikachu (surprised): Whoa!

== vanish
> Someone disappears where they stand: fading away (fade, the default), in the game's puff of smoke (smoke), a white flash (flash) or a vision (vision). They are gone from the scene after it.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
piplup: Pikachu? Where are you going?
pikachu vanish smoke
piplup (surprised): He's gone!

== crowd
> Sets out a crowd of one species, so many of them, around a spot; the group gets a name (krabby makes krabbys). A shape, a side to come from, fast… may follow.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
crowd krabby 5 right
pikachu (surprised): So many Krabby!
