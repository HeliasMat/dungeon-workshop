# The Wiki's pages: things held, given, put down; tools, crops (workshop/src/wiki/written.rs).

== hold
> Someone holds an item in their hands, its icon from the game (on the side they face); it follows their jumps and walks.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu hold Oran Berry
pikachu: For you!

== give
> An item passes from someone's hands to someone else's, through the air.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu hold Oran Berry
pikachu give Oran Berry to piplup
piplup: Thanks!

== drop
> Someone lets go of what they hold: it falls at their feet; at a place, it is set down there.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu hold Apple
pikachu drop
pikachu (surprised): Oops!

== throw
> Someone throws an item at someone (an arc, a shadow; it hits them, unless they catch it) or at a place, where it lands and stays.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
pikachu put left
piplup put right
pikachu hold Apple
pikachu throw Apple at piplup
piplup (pain): Hey!

== open
> A thing of the scene plays its animation up to its end: a chest opens.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
put object chest right_of pikachu
open chest
pikachu (joyous): It's open!

== close
> A thing of the scene goes back to its first picture: a chest closes.
scene: beach pikachu piplup
put object chest right_of pikachu
open chest
close chest
